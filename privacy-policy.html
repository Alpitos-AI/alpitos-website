Privacy Policy

How Alpitos handles website and communication data.

Effective date: March 20, 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Alpitos may collect, use, store, and disclose personal information when you visit alpitos.com, contact the team, subscribe to updates, or otherwise interact with the website.

This policy applies only to information handled through the website and related communications. It does not necessarily govern future blockchain products, wallets, token services, partner applications, or third-party platforms unless a separate policy expressly states that it does.

2. Information we may collect

We may collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, company name, and message contents when you contact us. We may also collect technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, referring URLs, and approximate location derived from network data.

We may also collect communication preferences, records of correspondence, fraud and abuse-prevention signals, and information you submit in connection with partnership requests, business inquiries, hiring discussions, waitlists, or community applications.

3. How we use information

Alpitos may use collected information to operate and improve the website, respond to inquiries, maintain security, analyze website performance, communicate updates, comply with legal obligations, and prepare for future ecosystem or service operations.

We may also use information to personalize content, maintain internal records, evaluate interest in specific ecosystem initiatives, investigate suspected misuse, defend legal claims, and enforce our terms, policies, and security procedures.

4. Cookies and analytics

The website may use cookies, log files, and similar technologies to remember preferences, measure engagement, and understand traffic patterns. You can manage cookies through your browser settings, although disabling them may affect some site functionality.

For more detail about the categories of cookies that may be used, please review the Cookie Policy available on this website.

5. Legal bases and compliance

Where applicable, Alpitos may process personal data based on consent, legitimate interests, contractual necessity, or legal obligations. Because the proposal states that final jurisdiction is still to be determined, compliance obligations may evolve as the operating entity and regulated offerings are established.

If the website later supports regulated services, onboarding, token-related features, or jurisdiction-specific flows, additional notices or supplemental disclosures may be provided at the point of collection.

6. Sharing of information

We may share information with service providers that support website hosting, communications, analytics, legal compliance, and security operations. We may also disclose information when required by law, regulation, court order, or to protect the rights, safety, and integrity of Alpitos, users, and the public.

In the event of a merger, acquisition, restructuring, financing transaction, or sale of assets, information may be disclosed as part of due diligence or transferred where permitted by law and subject to appropriate confidentiality measures.

7. Data retention

Personal information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of request, the sensitivity of the data, security requirements, records management needs, and applicable legal or regulatory obligations.

8. Data security and incident response

Alpitos uses reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction. These measures may include access controls, logging, vendor review, and environment-level safeguards appropriate to the nature of the website.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. While we work to protect information, we cannot guarantee absolute security and you provide information at your own risk.

9. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing of your personal information. You may also have the right to request a copy of the data we hold about you. Requests can be sent to admin@alpitos.com.

Where applicable, you may also have the right to withdraw consent, request portability, appeal certain decisions, or lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. We may need to verify your identity before responding to a request.

10. International transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own. Where required, we will use appropriate safeguards for cross-border transfers.

Because Alpitos may work with globally distributed infrastructure providers, contributors, and service partners, cross-border processing may occur even before a final operating jurisdiction is formally announced.

11. Third-party services and links

The website may reference or link to third-party sites, embedded content, wallets, analytics providers, community tools, or social platforms. Alpitos is not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties, and you should review their policies separately.

12. Children's privacy

The website is not directed to children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Your continued use of the website after an updated policy becomes effective indicates your acceptance of the revised policy to the extent permitted by law.

14. Contact

Privacy questions and requests should be sent to admin@alpitos.com.
