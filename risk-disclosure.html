Risk Disclosure

Important considerations for blockchain and digital asset projects.

Effective date: March 20, 2026

Project-stage risk

Alpitos is described in the proposal stage. Features, governance systems, funding, token mechanics, launch timelines, and technical architecture may change significantly or may not be completed as described.

Regulatory risk

Laws relating to digital assets, tokens, staking, decentralized governance, data services, and blockchain infrastructure vary across jurisdictions and may change rapidly. Future services may be delayed, modified, or restricted due to regulatory requirements.

Technology risk

Blockchain systems and smart contracts can contain bugs, vulnerabilities, design flaws, interoperability failures, and downtime risks. Network congestion, consensus failures, or software errors may disrupt functionality.

Security risk

Wallet compromises, key loss, phishing, malware, validator failures, infrastructure attacks, and malicious third-party activity may result in data loss, service outages, or loss of digital assets.

Market risk

Digital assets can be highly volatile and may lose substantial or all value. Utility, adoption, liquidity, and exchange availability may not develop as expected.

Operational risk

Ecosystem success depends on execution across product development, legal structure, fundraising, community building, and partnerships. Delays or failures in any of these areas may materially affect outcomes.

No financial advice

Nothing on this website should be interpreted as investment, legal, tax, or financial advice. You should perform your own diligence and consult qualified advisors before making any decision related to blockchain projects or digital assets.
