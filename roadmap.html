Roadmap

A phased rollout from foundation to full decentralization.

The proposal outlines a staged roadmap focused on technical architecture, launch readiness, adoption, enterprise onboarding, and eventual governance transition.

Phase 1

Foundation · 0 to 6 months

  • Finalize whitepaper, legal setup, and technical architecture.
  • Launch early platform infrastructure and begin developer outreach.
  • Secure seed funding.
Phase 2

Launch · 6 to 12 months

  • Launch the first public platform release.
  • Release three MVP applications.
  • Start contributor programs and community operations.
Phase 3

Growth · 12 to 24 months

  • Expand app categories.
  • Launch mobile SDKs.
  • Onboard enterprise partners.
  • Introduce broader community participation in platform direction.
Phase 4

Maturity · 2+ years

  • Scale into a mature contributor-driven operating model.
  • Expand integrations with major platforms and services.
  • Launch broader cross-service utility and collaboration features.
