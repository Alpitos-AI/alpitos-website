Ecosystem

A stack that connects applications, infrastructure, and incentives.

Alpitos is structured around a native token concept, an EVM-compatible chain, developer tooling, a diverse dApp portfolio, and revenue engines that reinforce long-term participation.

Native Token

The proposal describes a token used for transactions, governance, rewards, and staking, with demand linked to platform usage and transaction activity.

UtiliChain

An EVM-compatible blockchain concept focused on scalability, low fees, and an analytics layer for measuring utility and distributing rewards.

Developer Ecosystem

SDKs, APIs, smart contract libraries, grants, and hackathons support a builder network creating useful services for the platform.

App categories
