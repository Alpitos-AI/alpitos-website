Cookie Policy

How alpitos.com may use cookies and similar technologies.

Effective date: March 20, 2026

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help websites function properly, remember preferences, improve performance, and provide analytics.

2. Types of cookies we may use

Alpitos may use essential cookies for core site functionality, preference cookies to remember settings, performance cookies to understand how the site is used, and analytics cookies to measure traffic and engagement.

3. Third-party cookies

If third-party analytics, embedded media, or infrastructure providers are used, those services may set their own cookies subject to their policies.

4. Managing cookies

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect the functionality or performance of the website.

5. Changes

Alpitos may update this Cookie Policy as website functionality evolves. Updated versions will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

6. Contact

Questions about cookie usage can be sent to admin@alpitos.com.
